Lore

Claire Redfield is a current member of the human rights organization, TerraSave. She is the younger sister of BSAA operative and former S.T.A.R.S. member Chris Redfield. S ince her survival in the Raccoon City incident in 1998, Claire would become involved or end up in the midst of several biohazard outbreaks around the world, prompting her and her brother, to devote their career to combating the threat of Bio Organic Weapons.
