Lore

Ethan Winters (c.1984-10 February 2021) was an American mutant trained as a systems engineer. One of many mutant victims of biological weaponry, Winters was infected with a weaponized species of "Mold" during the 2017 Dulvey incident, caused by Eveline, an E-001 bioweapon created by The Connections and HCF. Following Eveline's death by his hands, Winters and his wife Mia were rescued by Chris Redfield of the Bioterrorism Security Assessment Alliance.
