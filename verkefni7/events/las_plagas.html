What Happened?


Following the Raccoon City Incident and subsequent outrage, the Umbrella Corporation was sanctioned by the United States Government and financially crippled, all but ceasing to exist as a business. Meanwhile, Leon S. Kennedy was recruited and trained by the government to become a special agent after they learned of his actions in Raccoon City. Now an agent for a special security agency, Leon is sent to Spain on a mission to rescue Ashley Graham, the daughter of President Graham, after she was kidnapped by a mysterious cult.
