Raccoon City Incident (Re2-Re3)

What Happened?


Two months after the events of Spencer Mansion (Resident Evil 1), the T-virus spread to Raccoon City by rats from a lab under the city. Leon Kennedy's first day as a police officer for the RPD was for lack of better words, a handful. He fought through many hoards of zombies, was stalked by Mr. X, and fought a biological weapon by the name of G.
He met Ada Wong after one of his encounters with Mr. X.
Ada Wong was in Raccoon city because she was hired to steal the T-virus and sell it to the highest bidder
Claire Redfield was looking for her brother that went missing after the events of Spencer Mansion and went through pretty much the same things that Leon went through. Though she rescued a little girl by the name of Sherry Birkins that was held captive by Chief Irons in the Raccoon City Orphanage.
Jill Valentine was in Raccoon City in her apartment when she was attacked by Nemesis which was made to neutralize the S.T.A.R.S. members.
