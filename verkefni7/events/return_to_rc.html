Return To Raccoon City

What Happened?

A body has surfaced at an abandoned hotel in the Midwest, the latest in a mysterious string of deaths throughout the United States. FBI intelligence analyst Grace Ashcroft has been assigned to the case at the same location where her mother was murdered eight years ago. However, when reports emerge that a police officer has gone missing from the hotel, veteran agent Leon S. Kennedy is also dispatched to the scene. As Grace and Leon's paths converge, they must face their pasts and uncover the truth behind the Raccoon City Incident that changed the world forever.
